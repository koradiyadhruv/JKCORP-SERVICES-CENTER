About JKCORP Service Center

We help rural families navigate government documentation and digital services. JKCORP Service Center assists with Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, driving license, passport, farmer schemes and scholarships. We provide Remote eKYC Processing, Aadhaar-linked Digital Verification and Online Document Processing to minimize physical visits while keeping your data secure.
